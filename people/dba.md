# 🗄️ Carol Query — DBA

**Carol Query**, the DBA, joins the bridge and sends graphs showing a healthy database.

Queries complete quickly once they arrive. Carol points out that the timeout is occurring in the application **before a database connection is obtained**.

> The database isn't slow. Your application is waiting to talk to it.

This is a useful observation delivered with just enough satisfaction to be annoying.
